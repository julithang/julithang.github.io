My Journey

As a child, I spent my free time creating comics, which eventually manifested into insistently doodling throughout school. Once I had access to a computer and the internet, I spent my free time playing around with HTML/CSS on Neopets, and then Tumblr. Eventually, I explored other creative digital mediums such as filming and then editing videos in iMovie as well as graphic design in Photoshop and Gimp.

My interest in technology and the arts eventually led me to pursue UX design, where I can feed my curiosity about human behavior and advocate for human needs.
